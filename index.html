Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about integrating, configuring, or safely using FalconLine? Find descriptive answers here.

How does FalconLine work?

FalconLine is an advanced server-orchestration agent designed specifically for Discord's API. It uses natural language processing to map verbal or descriptive prompts (e.g., "Minecraft Server" or "Tech Academy") into logical server architecture nodes. It compiles custom categories, establishes styled channels with proper emojis, designs structured role groupings, and creates those elements in sequence directly inside your authorized Discord guild.

Is FalconLine free?

Yes. FalconLine is completely free. We believe that community builders, content creators, and independent developers should have immediate access to world-class organization utilities without being blocked by paid plans, subscriptions, token paywalls, or premium-only templates. All features, commands, and options are fully available to every user.

Does it support existing servers?

Yes. FalconLine is capable of compiling and building structures inside any existing server where you have administrative integration permission. It will build and nest the new categories, channels, and roles alongside your pre-existing content without modifying or deleting any active channels, unless specifically requested.

Can I reset my server?

Yes. Running the `/reset` command in your server allows you to wipe bot-created channels quickly and cleanly. For safety and security, this command requires immediate confirmation from the server owner. Once confirmed, FalconLine will safely remove the categories, text/voice channels, and role nodes it generated so you can execute a completely clean template rebuild.

How long does setup take?

The entire configuration is virtually instantaneous. Authorizing the bot via our secure link takes less than 10 seconds. From there, issuing your `/setup` command and watching the server populate with beautiful categories, channels, and roles takes between 5 to 10 seconds in total.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 17, 2026

Welcome to FalconLine. We are committed to maintaining the highest standards of data protection and privacy for our users. This Privacy Policy details how our application collects, uses, and safeguards information when you integrate FalconLine into your Discord guild.

1. Data We Do Not Collect

Unlike complex analytics crawlers, FalconLine does not monitor, index, store, or log user messages, chat histories, personal files, voice logs, user identifiers, or metadata outside the active Discord API payload. The bot operates solely as a "Structure Generator" on a strict state-to-state session model.

2. Ephemeral Generation Scopes

When executing the /setup command, our systems temporarily parse your request string to extract community roles, categories, and channels. This data is handled in memory, compiled, and dispatched to Discord's official Gateway endpoints. No database caches or persistent records of your specific layout configurations are preserved on our servers.

3. Third-Party Interactions (Discord Inc.)

FalconLine processes setups directly inside Discord's application framework. Your interaction is subject to Discord's general Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. All administrative actions (channel creations, role sorting) are fully logged in your server's official Discord Audit Log, ensuring complete transparency.

4. Data Deletion

Running /reset immediately triggers the deletion of all bot-generated channels and roles. No residuals remain on FalconLine's runtime systems, ensuring you maintain absolute control over your server space.

5. Contact and Inquiries

For any formal inquiries regarding security protocols or our compliance patterns, please contact our support desk at nationserver.yt@gmail.com.
